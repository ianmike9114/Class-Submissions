How this page works
  1. Pick a subject and lesson in the Course outline, then tap an assignment to open it.
  2. Read the lesson material that opens on the right — it stays on this page, just scroll inside it.
  3. Submit your work: paste a share link, or take/upload photos of your pages.
  4. Check back later for your grade and your teacher's feedback.
  5. Already graded but need to fix it? Open it and tap Request to redo — your teacher can reopen it.
