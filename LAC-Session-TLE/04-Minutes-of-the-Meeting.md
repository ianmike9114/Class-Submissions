## Discussion

The Facilitator, Ms. Angelica P. Reyes, opened by explaining the LMS
runs on Firebase Auth and Firestore only (Google Sign-In, no passwords,
no cost) and that students submit a **link** — Google Doc/PDF, Drive,
GitHub Gist/CodePen, or YouTube — rather than uploading files, avoiding
the need for paid storage.

She demonstrated: creating a subject and section, generating a
join-code/QR code for students to self-enroll, creating an assignment
with a total-points score, reviewing a sample submission, encoding a
single raw score, and publishing it so the student immediately sees
the result on their end. Questions raised by members centered on
whether non-Gmail students can join (no — Google Sign-In only) and
whether teachers' subjects stay private from each other (yes — each
teacher's subjects/sections/assignments/submissions/enrollments are
isolated to their own account).

During the workshop, all 8 teachers created a test subject/section on
their own accounts. Ms. Bea Marie S. Fernandez and Mr. Paolo R. Aquino
shared their setups during the sharing segment.

## Decisions/Agreements
